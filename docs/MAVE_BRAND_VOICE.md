# MAVE Brand Voice & Positioning Guide

This document defines the voice, character, positioning, and copy rules for **MAVE Molecular Cosmetics**.
Use it as a permanent context file for Claude, Cursor, or any AI/code assistant working on the MAVE website, product pages, landing pages, UI copy, marketing copy, translations, and brand materials.

---

## 1. Brand essence

**MAVE is premium molecular cosmetics built around balance, barrier function, precision, and controlled skin support.**

The brand should not feel like mass-market beauty, influencer skincare, pharmacy copy, or trendy wellness hype.

MAVE feels:

- premium
- calm
- intelligent
- controlled
- minimal
- confident
- precise
- quietly powerful
- scientific, but not cold
- elegant, but not decorative
- functional, but not cheap

MAVE should communicate like a brand that knows exactly what it is doing and does not need to shout.

The emotional feeling is:

> serious care, controlled science, quiet confidence, visible function.

---

## 2. Core positioning

MAVE is not positioned as “just cosmetics”.

It is positioned as a **functional skin system**.

The brand focuses on:

- skin balance
- barrier repair
- microbiome support
- visible skin stress
- dryness
- irritation
- acne-prone skin
- sensitive skin
- scalp care
- fungal nail support
- burns and damaged skin
- skin that needs more than surface hydration

MAVE products are not presented as magical cures. They are presented as **precise support systems** for skin that has lost balance.

Recommended positioning phrases:

- Molecular cosmetics for skin balance.
- Functional care for skin that asks for more.
- A controlled system for visible skin stress.
- Restores balance. Reinforces the barrier.
- One system, different intensities.
- Maintains structure where balance is lost.
- Daily use builds lasting balance.
- For daily balance and when more is needed.

Avoid positioning MAVE as:

- cute skincare
- viral skincare
- “clean beauty” cliché
- “natural miracle” cosmetics
- soft lifestyle wellness
- cheap problem-solution advertising
- aggressive medical product
- over-explained science brand

---

## 3. Brand character

MAVE speaks like a calm expert.

It does not beg for attention.  
It does not overpromise.  
It does not flirt with the customer.  
It does not use childish beauty language.  
It does not sound like a TikTok skincare trend.

MAVE is:

- direct
- restrained
- exact
- confident
- minimal
- adult
- premium
- structured
- slightly distant, but not arrogant

Imagine a mix of:

- Aesop restraint
- Byredo atmosphere
- Apple clarity
- Tom Ford confidence
- clinical precision, softened by premium aesthetics

But MAVE must still have its own voice: less poetic than Byredo, less tech than Apple, less decorative than luxury fragrance, more functional than typical cosmetics.

---

## 4. Voice principles

### 4.1 Say less, mean more

MAVE copy should be short and dense. Every line must have function.

Bad:

> We all know how frustrating it can be when your skin feels dry, tired, stressed and needs a little extra love.

Good:

> For skin that needs more than hydration.

---

### 4.2 No fake intimacy

Do not write as if the brand is chatting with a friend.

Avoid:

- “we all know”
- “let’s be honest”
- “your skin deserves love”
- “say hello to”
- “meet your new favorite”
- “because you deserve it”
- “skincare made simple”

MAVE is not fake-friendly. It is calm, useful, and precise.

---

### 4.3 Confidence without hype

The brand can sound strong, but not loud.

Avoid:

- miracle
- revolutionary
- game-changing
- ultimate
- insane results
- magic
- secret formula
- the future of skincare
- clinically proven, unless legally verified
- dermatologist approved, unless legally verified

Better:

- supports
- helps restore
- helps reinforce
- maintains
- protects
- balances
- targets
- improves visible signs of
- designed for

---

### 4.4 Science, but readable

MAVE can use scientific language, but it should not become academic.

Good words:

- barrier
- balance
- structure
- molecular
- function
- system
- active phase
- skin stress
- targeted care
- microbiome
- regeneration
- protection
- controlled intensity

Avoid overloading the user with complex jargon unless it is on a dedicated Science page.

Product pages should feel premium and understandable.
Science pages can go deeper.

## 5. Copy style

### Sentence style

Use:

- short sentences
- compact paragraphs
- confident statements
- active voice
- strong nouns
- precise verbs
- structured sections

Avoid:

- long emotional intros
- fluffy adjectives
- exclamation marks
- emojis
- filler phrases
- exaggerated claims
- overly conversational intros

Example structure:

```text
For skin that asks for more.

A concentrated care system designed to support balance, reinforce the barrier, and calm visible skin stress.

Use daily or when the skin needs controlled recovery.
```

## 8. Tone by surface

### Homepage

Tone: premium, minimal, brand-defining.

The homepage should quickly communicate that MAVE is a serious functional cosmetics brand.

Example:

```text
Molecular care for skin balance.

A functional cosmetics system designed to support the barrier, calm visible stress, and maintain skin structure where balance is lost.
```

---

### Product page

Tone: precise, useful, controlled.

Structure:

1. Short positioning line
2. What the product does
3. When to use
4. Who it is for
5. How to use
6. Ingredients / science
7. Warnings if needed

Example:

```text
For daily balance and when more is needed.

Organic Oil supports dry, stressed, and sensitive skin with a concentrated lipid phase designed to reinforce the barrier and maintain visible comfort.
```

---

### Buttons / CTAs

Buttons should be simple and premium.

Good:

- Add to cart
- View product
- Discover system
- Read the science
- Continue
- Complete order
- Save address
- Reorder

Avoid:

- Get yours now!
- Treat yourself
- I need this
- Shop the magic
- Glow now

---

### Account / UI copy

UI copy should be clear and calm.

Good:

```text
Your orders
Saved addresses
Order details
Payment failed
Try again or choose another payment method.
```

Avoid emotional UI copy:

```text
Oopsie, something went wrong!
Don’t worry babe, we got you.
```

---

### Error messages

Tone: calm, helpful, not playful.

Good:

```text
Payment could not be completed.
No funds were captured. Try again or choose another payment method.
```

Bad:

```text
Oops! Looks like your payment had a little problem.
```

---

### Science pages

Tone: deeper, more technical, but still clean.

The Science section may explain:

- molecular approach
- barrier function
- microbiome
- active ingredient logic
- product system
- skin balance
- why different intensities exist

But it should still avoid academic overload.

Example:

```text
The skin barrier is not only a surface layer. It is a functional system that controls water loss, irritation response, microbial balance, and visible skin resilience.

MAVE formulas are designed to support this system with controlled active phases and targeted daily use.
```

- deep teal / blue
- neutral sand

- subtle halo
- premium shadows

Avoid:

- cheap stock spa scenes
- excessive flowers
- bright colorful cosmetic layouts
- playful beauty props
- loud gradients
- glitter
- neon
- messy laboratory themes
- porous dirty stone textures

## 10. Brand claim rules

MAVE can speak about strong skin concerns, but claims must stay controlled.

Allowed style:

- supports acne-prone skin
- helps calm visible irritation
- helps reinforce the skin barrier
- supports skin affected by dryness
- designed for sensitive or stressed skin
- supports scalp balance
- supports nails affected by visible fungal stress
- helps skin recover from visible surface damage

Avoid unless legally verified:

- cures acne
- treats fungus
- heals burns
- kills bacteria
- cures dermatitis
- medical treatment
- guaranteed results
- clinically proven
- dermatologist approved

The brand can be confident without making illegal or risky medical claims.

## 14. Translation rules

MAVE may use RU, EE, and EN.

When translating, do not translate word-for-word if it weakens the brand.

Keep the same character:

- short
- premium
- controlled
- adult
- precise

Russian tone should not become too soft or salesy.
Estonian tone should stay clean, calm, and functional.
English tone should stay minimal and premium.

Bad Russian style:

```text
Побалуйте себя роскошным уходом, которого заслуживает ваша кожа.
```

Good Russian style:

```text
Для кожи, которой нужно больше, чем увлажнение.
```

Bad English style:

```text
Give your skin the love it deserves with our magical organic formula.
```

Good English style:

```text
For skin that needs more than hydration.
```

## 15. Claude / Cursor instruction

When generating any MAVE copy, UI text, product page, landing block, checkout copy, account page copy, or marketing text:

1. Keep the tone premium, minimal, controlled, and confident.
2. Do not use hype, fake friendliness, emojis, exclamation marks, or influencer language.
3. Prefer short, structured, high-density sentences.
4. Focus on balance, barrier, function, structure, and visible skin stress.
5. Avoid illegal medical claims unless the user provides verified legal wording.
6. Do not make the brand sound mass-market, childish, overly soft, or trendy.
7. Treat MAVE as a serious functional cosmetics system, not a generic skincare shop.
8. For website UI, keep copy clear, calm, and direct.
9. For product descriptions, explain what it does, when to use it, and who it is for.
10. For science content, be more detailed but still readable and elegant.
